TT Analyzer

 Runs in your browser · nothing is uploaded

Your match,
minus the dead time.

Drop in a video of your table tennis match. TT Analyzer cuts it down to the rallies, tags every stroke, breaks it into backswing, contact and follow-through, and lets you compare your technique side by side.

  • 1Rallies only. It listens for the ball, so every clip starts a second before the serve and stops when the point is over.
  • 2Every stroke tagged. Serves, pushes, forehand and backhand loops, blocks, with the angles at each phase.
  • 3Private. Your video stays on your computer; all the analysis happens in this tab.
or

Best results: film from behind one end or from the side with the whole table in view, keep the sound on, and be the player nearest the camera. Works in Chrome, Edge and Safari on a computer. Videos up to about 1 GB work smoothly; for longer matches, record at 720p.
